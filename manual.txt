INTRODUCTION & GETTING STARTED

About BrightAdel BrightAdel is a secure web-based hostel booking and management platform built for the University of Cape Coast (UCC) community. It connects students searching for accommodation, hostel managers listing properties, and platform administrators overseeing operations.


What You Need
•	A smartphone or computer with an internet browser.
•	A valid Ghanaian mobile number for SMS verification.
•	A Mobile Money wallet or bank card for payments.


Account Types
•	Student: search, book, and pay for hostel slots.
•	Hostel Manager: list and manage hostels, rooms, receipts, and payouts.
•	Administrator: approve applications, manage users, and monitor the platform.


Registering as a Student
1.	Open the BrightAdel website and click Create Account.
2.	Enter your first name, last name, phone number, and password.
3.	Select Student and submit.
4.	You will receive a 6-digit code by SMS.
5.	Enter the code on the verification page within 10 minutes.
6.	Once verified, log in with your phone number and password.


Logging In
1.	Click Login.
2.	Enter your phone number and password.
3.	You will be taken straight to your dashboard.


Resetting a Forgotten Password
1.	Click Forgot Password on the login page.
2.	Enter your registered phone number.
3.	Enter the SMS code you receive.
4.	Set a new password and log in.


Security Tips
•	Never share your OTP code with anyone.
•	BrightAdel staff will never ask for your password.
•	Always confirm that your receipt carries a verification code.


STUDENT GUIDE

Searching for Hostels Use the homepage search bar and filters: location (Ayensu, Kwaprow, Amamoma, Old Site), room type, price range, amenities to exclude, air-conditioning, and available slots. Sort results by name, price, or rating.

Viewing Hostel Details Click any hostel card to see photos, room types, prices, available slots, a map with GPS directions, and student reviews.


Booking a Room
1.	Log in as a student.
2.	Open a hostel and choose a room type with available slots.
3.	Click Book, add an optional note, and confirm.
4.	You will be taken to the payment page.


Paying for Your Booking
•	Pay at least half to confirm your slot, or pay in full.
•	Click Pay to open the secure Paystack checkout.
•	Approve the charge with Mobile Money or card.
•	Once verified, your booking becomes Confirmed and a receipt with a verification code is generated.
•	Note: total charge = hostel fee + GH₵20 platform fee + 2% gateway charge.


Managing Your Bookings (My Bookings)
•	View statuses: pending, confirmed, checked in, checked out.
•	Cancel unpaid bookings.
•	Leave Hostel frees your slot when you move out.
•	If your manager requests a checkout, you may Approve or Decline it.


Your Receipt Every payment produces a receipt with a unique code. Managers use this code to confirm your payment is genuine at check-in.

Reviewing a Hostel After checking out, open the booking and click Write a Review, rate 1–5 stars, and add a comment.

Notifications Click the bell icon to see updates on bookings, payments, and checkouts; mark them read when done.


HOSTEL MANAGER GUIDE

Becoming a Manager
1.	On the register page, choose Manager.
2.	Enter your personal details, hostel name, and location.
3.	Upload a proof-of-ownership document (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF).
4.	Wait for admin approval — you will be notified once approved.


Manager Dashboard See your total slots, booked slots, available slots, and every booking across your hostels.

Adding a Hostel
1.	Click Add Hostel.
2.	Enter the name, location, paid amenities (water, electricity, Wi-Fi), and AC availability.
3.	Optionally capture GPS coordinates with the location button for the map.
4.	Save.


Uploading Photos On the hostel edit page, upload photos under the labels: Cover (Compound), Bathroom, Bedroom, and Kitchen. Delete outdated photos at any time.


Adding Rooms
1.	Click Add Room.
2.	Choose the hostel, room type (1–4 in a room), and quantity.
3.	Set the price per year. Slots are calculated automatically (quantity × capacity).


Managing Bookings
•	Check In confirmed (paid) students when they arrive.
•	Request Checkout from checked-in students at the end of the session — the student must approve it.


Verifying Receipts Open Verify Receipt, enter the code on a student’s receipt, and confirm it matches a real booking before check-in. Reject any receipt that fails verification.


Receipts & Reports
•	Receipts lists all paid bookings with their codes.
•	Export CSV downloads your bookings as a spreadsheet.


Payouts
1.	Open Payouts to see your available balance.
2.	Enter an amount, choose MoMo or Bank, and enter the destination account.
3.	Submit — you will be notified when the admin pays it.


ADMINISTRATOR GUIDE

Admin Dashboard View platform statistics: students, managers, hostels, rooms, bookings, total processed value, and service earnings. Review manager earnings, hostel performance, recent bookings, and announcements.


Approving Manager Applications
1.	Open Applications.
2.	Review the applicant’s details and open the uploaded ownership document.
3.	Click Approve to create the manager account, or Reject to delete the application.


Managing Payouts
1.	Open Payouts to see pending requests.
2.	After sending the money by MoMo or bank transfer, click Mark as Paid.
3.	To decline a request, click Reject and add a note. The manager is notified in both cases.


Announcements
•	Publish: enter a title and message; it appears on the homepage.
•	Delete: remove outdated announcements.


Managing Users
1.	Open Users to see all accounts.
2.	Suspend blocks a user from logging in (click again to reactivate).
3.	Delete permanently removes accounts that have no bookings or hostels.


Removing Hostels Admins may delete hostels that have no bookings. Hostels with active bookings are protected — suspend the manager instead.

Exporting Data Use Export CSV to download booking records for audits and reporting.


Fraud Prevention
•	Verify suspicious receipts with the Verify Receipt tool.
•	Suspend accounts involved in fraudulent activity.
•	Monitor revenue figures on the dashboard for unusual patterns.


TROUBLESHOOTING, FAQ & SUPPORT


Common Problems & Fixes
•	No OTP SMS arriving: wait 60 seconds and click Resend Code; check your network; confirm you entered the correct phone number.
•	Payment made but booking still pending: return to the site using Paystack’s return link so verification completes. If unresolved, contact support with your payment reference.
•	Forgotten password: use Forgot Password with your phone number and the SMS code.
•	Receipt code shows “not found”: re-enter the code exactly as printed (8 characters, capital letters). If it still fails, the receipt may be fake — report it.
•	Cannot log in: check that your account is not suspended and that Caps Lock is off.


Frequently Asked Questions
•	Does BrightAdel take commission from managers? No. Managers receive 100% of the hostel fee; students pay the GH₵20 platform fee + 2% gateway charge.
•	When is my slot guaranteed? Once payment is verified and your booking shows Confirmed.
•	Can a manager remove me from my room? No — checkout requires your approval.
•	Can I review a hostel while staying in it? Reviews open only after checkout.

Glossary
•	OTP: one-time 6-digit SMS code.
•	Slot: one bed space in a room.
•	Receipt code: unique 8-character proof of a genuine payment.
•	Payout: transfer of a manager’s earned funds.
•	Checkout: final departure that frees a slot.
