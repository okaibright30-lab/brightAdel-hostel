How BrightAdel works

One platform connecting students, hostel managers and the admin — safely and transparently.

🎓 For Students

  1. Create an account with your phone number and verify it with the SMS code.
  2. Search hostels by location, price, room type, amenities and map.
  3. Book a slot and pay at least half online (MoMo / card).
  4. Get a receipt with a verification code. Check in. Review after checkout.

You can book multiple hostels and leave anytime — your slot frees up automatically.

🏠 For Managers

  1. Apply with your name, phone and a proof-of-ownership document.
  2. Admin reviews and approves your application.
  3. Add your hostel, rooms, photos and map location.
  4. Receive bookings, check students in, and keep 100% of your hostel fees.

Check-outs require the student's approval — no secret evictions.

🛡️ For Everyone (Trust)

  • Payments are processed securely by Paystack.
  • Every receipt carries a verification code managers can check.
  • Managers are verified with ownership documents.
  • The admin can suspend users and remove fraudulent hostels.

Fees: students add a GH₵ 20.00 service fee + 2% gateway fee per payment. Managers receive 100% of hostel fees.
